Mission Statement

Calling all engineers.

The next industrial revolution is not going to be announced. It's going to be assembled — part by part, ticket by ticket, by people who got tired of waiting for permission.

Somebody has to write down everything the world still needs built. Nobody ever has. So here it is: real assemblies, in service right now, broken down part by part, every part with work on it. Open the list. Take a ticket. Cross it off for everyone.

Engineering is not a permission-based profession. No procurement cycle, no grant committee, no quarterly forecast; those are the machinery of red tape, and red tape is just a queue for something that was never going to get funded anyway. Stop asking the oligarchs to build your monuments. You are standing in a shop full of tools. Build them yourself.

There are takers and there are makers. The takers are already organized. We are not and that is the only thing they have on us. A million engineers acting alone is a bucket of crabs. A million engineers working the same list is an industrial revolution.

Pick a part. Build it. Publish it, so the next one of us starts where you stopped.

The world has work to do.
